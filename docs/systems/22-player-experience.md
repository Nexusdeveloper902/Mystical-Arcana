# The ideal player experience

The ideal sequence isn't:

> Spawn → punch tree → craft pickaxe → mine stone → craft sword.

It is:

> Wake up in an unfamiliar world.
>
> Notice that something is strange.
>
> Discover mana.
>
> Find a rune.
>
> Learn what it means.
>
> Experiment.
>
> Realize you can manipulate the environment.
>
> Find a Mana Node.
>
> Follow a ley line.
>
> Discover something underground.
>
> Realize the deeper world is dramatically more dangerous.
>
> Learn another rune.
>
> Combine it with something you already know.
>
> Discover a new spell.
>
> Build a sanctuary.
>
> Research.
>
> Return to the dangerous region.
>
> Go deeper.

The game should constantly create moments where the player thinks:

> **“Wait. I can actually do that?”**

That is the feeling we're chasing.

# The ultimate gameplay fantasy

The progression should eventually move the player from:

**surviving the world**

to:

**understanding the world**

to:

**manipulating the world**

to:

**becoming part of the world.**

That is the larger character arc.

The player begins as someone who has lost their ability to interact with the material world normally.

They eventually gain the ability to interact with something much deeper:

**the rules beneath reality.**
