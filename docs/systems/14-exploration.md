# Exploration loop

The intended loop is roughly:

**Explore**

↓

**Observe**

↓

**Discover magical phenomenon**

↓

**Gather resources / knowledge**

↓

**Experiment with runes**

↓

**Create spells**

↓

**Reach more dangerous regions**

↓

**Encounter corruption**

↓

**Return / establish sanctuary**

↓

**Research**

↓

**Increase magical capability**

↓

**Explore deeper**

This is the core gameplay loop.

# The world as a mystery

The player shouldn't immediately understand everything.

The world contains remnants of previous Arcanists.

Ruins.

Experiments.

Forgotten knowledge.

Strange phenomena.

Environmental clues.

The player gradually reconstructs what happened.

So exploration is simultaneously:

**survival + archaeology + magical research.**

# Environmental storytelling

There shouldn't need to be an NPC standing around explaining everything.

The environment itself should communicate history.

For example:

A ruined magical structure.

↓

A strange rune.

↓

A damaged mana node.

↓

An unusual creature.

↓

A research chamber.

↓

A clue about what the previous Arcanists were doing.

This makes discovery meaningful.
