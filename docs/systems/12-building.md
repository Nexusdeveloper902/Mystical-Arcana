# Base building

Building exists, but it follows the game's magical philosophy.

The player uses **stabilization magic** to establish structures.

The planned system includes:

* buildable structures
* resources as construction inputs
* stabilization rune
* structure persistence
* stability meter



So the player isn't constructing a normal house.

They're **anchoring a piece of stable reality inside an unstable world.**

# Sanctuaries

Sanctuaries are the player's safe magical spaces.

They provide:

* storage
* crafting
* research
* spell creation
* magical experimentation
* mana stabilization
* increased regeneration
* protection from corruption



And aesthetically:

> **The outside world is chaos. The sanctuary is harmony.**

The original vision describes rune circles humming in harmony, floating crafting tables, responsive crystals, and a feeling of rhythm and stillness.
