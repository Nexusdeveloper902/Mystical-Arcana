# Persistence

The world should eventually remember what the player does.

The planned save system stores things such as:

* player stats
* learned runes
* world seed
* placed bases

with autosaves and manual saves. 

This is important because the player's relationship with the world should feel persistent.

Their sanctuary isn't temporary.

Their knowledge isn't temporary.

Their world isn't disposable.
