# Long-term vision

The eventual vision extends beyond a purely solitary game.

The original document describes the possibility of multiplayer magical conclaves where players could combine their ley anchors and collectively shape the world. The world's mana could react dynamically to cooperation or greed. 

That means the underlying systems should ideally remain compatible with a future where:

* multiple players establish sanctuaries
* ley anchors interact
* mana networks respond collectively
* players cooperate on magical infrastructure
* collective actions change the world

But **multiplayer is future vision, not the foundation of the current game.**

The solo experience has to stand on its own.
