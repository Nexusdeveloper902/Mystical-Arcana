# The world

The world is **fractured between serenity and chaos**.

Ancient ley lines run beneath the landscape.

Mana exists throughout the environment.

The farther the player travels into increasingly dense magical regions, the more unstable reality becomes. 

The world is therefore divided into progressively more magical ecological layers.

## Surface

Forests.

Plains.

Rivers.

Mountains.

Basic resources.

Relatively safe exploration.

## Subterranean / crystalline regions

Caves.

Mana deposits.

Crystalline formations.

Stronger magical phenomena.

More dangerous creatures.

## Deep regions

Bioluminescent environments.

Highly concentrated mana.

Corruption.

Unstable physics.

Powerful enemies.

## Ancient sanctuaries / floating regions

Remnants of the old Arcanists.

Advanced magical architecture.

Lost knowledge.

Extremely powerful magical phenomena.

The vertical structure is explicitly part of the planned world design.

# Procedural generation

The world is ultimately intended to be **procedurally generated and seed-based**.

The planned generator uses:

* layered noise
* vertical tiers
* biome variation
* seeded generation
* ley-line placement
* mana-density variation
* resource rules
* enemy rules



The important thing is that procedural generation shouldn't merely change terrain.

It changes **the magical ecology**.

Higher mana density can mean:

* rarer resources
* stronger enemies
* more mana nodes
* more dangerous phenomena
* different environmental appearance

The world should therefore feel like a system rather than a randomly assembled map.

# Ley Lines

Ley lines are the world's magical circulatory system.

They are essentially **invisible rivers of mana** connecting different areas.

Their eventual representation is planned as splines.

They influence:

* mana intensity
* Mana Node distribution
* environmental magic
* potentially resource distribution
* potentially enemy presence
* future world-state mechanics



Visually, ley lines can become visible when mana concentration is high enough.

This creates an important exploration mechanic:

> **Follow the magical geography.**

The player can discover where the world is powerful by learning to read it.

# Mana Nodes

Mana Nodes are natural conduits.

They provide the player with access to concentrated mana and increase regeneration when nearby.

The current development plan treats them as an early core mechanic, with:

* proximity-based mana regeneration
* subtle visual glow
* magical ambience/humming



But conceptually they are much more than recharge stations.

They are **physical manifestations of the world's magical network**.

Eventually they can become strategic points within the world.
