# The visual identity

The visual identity is **stylized mystical fantasy**, not realistic fantasy.

The technical roadmap recommends **Unity URP** specifically for a stylized/performance-oriented presentation. The environment direction calls for low-poly environmental assets such as trees and rocks. 

The visual language should therefore be:

### Natural + magical

The base world should use recognizable natural materials:

* stone
* dirt
* wood
* leaves
* grass
* water
* crystals

Then mana progressively transforms them.

The magic should feel like it has **grown into the world**, not been placed on top of it.

A normal tree might eventually develop crystalline leaves.

A river can shimmer with residual mana.

A cavern can become saturated with magical light.

A rock formation can contain mana crystals.

This principle comes directly from the vision: mana changes the rules of matter and produces phenomena such as crystalline vegetation and corrupted caverns.

# Color philosophy

The game should have a **natural, earthy foundation** with controlled magical colors.

Think:

**earth → vegetation → stone → water → crystal → mana**

rather than:

**everything → neon purple.**

Magic needs contrast.

Mana should be visibly luminous, but it shouldn't simply blow everything out to white.

This is particularly important for Mana Nodes: their glow should communicate **energy and life**, not look like an overexposed light source.

Magical effects can become spectacular when appropriate, but normal environmental magic should remain subtle enough that the player can appreciate the environment around it.

# Lighting

Lighting should carry a huge portion of the atmosphere.

### Surface

Soft natural lighting.

Sunlight.

Long shadows.

Fog.

Subtle magical illumination.

### Underground

Increasingly dependent on:

* crystals
* mana deposits
* bioluminescence
* magical phenomena

### High-mana areas

Lighting becomes increasingly unnatural.

The player should be able to *feel* that something is wrong before understanding why.

### Sanctuaries

The opposite.

Warm, stable, controlled, rhythmic.

The outside world is chaotic.

The sanctuary is calm.
