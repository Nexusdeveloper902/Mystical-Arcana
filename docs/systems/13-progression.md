# Progression

Progression is intended to resemble enlightenment.

At the beginning:

> Magic barely responds.

Eventually:

> The player understands mana.

Then:

> They manipulate it deliberately.

Eventually:

> They reshape the world around themselves.

The original vision explicitly describes the player beginning weak and gradually gaining the ability to reshape their surroundings through discovered knowledge.
