# Sound identity

Sound should follow the same duality as the visuals.

### Nature

* wind
* water
* leaves
* caves
* wildlife
* footsteps

### Magic

* low hums
* resonant tones
* crystalline sounds
* rune activation
* magical pulses
* spell impacts

### Mana instability

* distorted resonance
* increasingly intense magical ambience
* environmental audio changes
* storms

### Sanctuary

Quiet.

Stable.

Resonant.

The player should hear the difference between:

**“I am safe.”**

and

**“Something is very wrong here.”**

# Atmosphere

The intended emotional spectrum is:

**wonder → serenity → curiosity → awe → danger → unease → melancholy**

The world should feel beautiful enough that the player wants to explore it.

But there should always be the sense that something ancient happened here.

Something has been forgotten.

And something about mana isn't entirely safe.

The original tone explicitly balances wonder and serenity against the quiet fear of losing oneself to the force that enables survival.
