# Combat

Combat is deliberate rather than mindless.

The player should feel:

> “I am manipulating something dangerous.”

rather than:

> “I'm clicking until the enemy dies.”

Combat is based on magical control.

Spells can:

* damage
* protect
* manipulate
* interrupt
* potentially alter positioning
* interact with enemies differently depending on their magical nature

The vision explicitly describes combat as a dance of light and motion and emphasizes that magic should be respected rather than treated simply as a weapon.

# Enemies

Enemies are manifestations of **corrupted mana**.

That means enemy design should not simply be:

> wolf with more HP.

Creatures should visually communicate magical corruption.

Potential forms established by the vision include:

* corrupted beasts
* living crystal entities
* smoke/light entities
* twisted magical creatures



The development plan includes:

* Idle
* Chase
* Attack
* Flee
* health
* damage
* knockback
* loot
* corruption behavior



And deeper environments can produce increasingly corrupted behavior.

# Mana corruption

Enemy corruption should be directly related to world depth and mana density.

Higher-tier creatures can have:

* area effects
* debuffs
* transformations
* unusual behaviors



This means the environment explains the enemy.

The deeper you go:

**more mana → more instability → more corruption → more dangerous creatures.**
