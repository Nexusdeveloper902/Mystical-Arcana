# Schematics

Schematics are the player's learned magical recipes.

They represent:

> **“I understand this magical construction.”**

The player can discover or research them.

Once learned, they allow the player to reliably reproduce a spell.

This creates two distinct forms of magic:

### Improvisation

The player experiments.

### Knowledge

The player uses a known schematic.

That distinction is directly supported by the original vision's concept of spells being either improvised or pre-formed from schematics.

# Crafting

Crafting is not merely:

> 3 wood + 2 stone = table.

It should increasingly become:

> resources + magical knowledge + runes = new capability.

The Research Interface is intended to let players use resources and runes to create new schematics or upgrade runes. 

Thus crafting becomes an extension of magical research.

# Research

Research is the intellectual progression system.

The player discovers:

* runes
* combinations
* schematics
* upgrades
* knowledge about the world

This reinforces the theme that **understanding is power**.
